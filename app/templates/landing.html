Private by design

Face data is sensitive biometric data, so we treat it that way.

  • No photos stored. Faces become an encrypted 128-number template that cannot be turned back into a picture.
  • Consent first. Nobody's face can be registered without recorded consent, and consent forms are included.
  • Automatic deletion. Templates of people who leave or stop coming are deleted on a schedule you choose.
  • Right of access. Each person can download everything stored about them in one click.
  • Your server, if you prefer. The self-hosted edition keeps every byte inside your own network.
