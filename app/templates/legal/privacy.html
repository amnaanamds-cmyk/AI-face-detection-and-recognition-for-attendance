Privacy policy

{{ s.legal_company }}, {{ s.legal_address }} ("we") operates {{ app_name }} (the "Service"). Organizations such as schools, companies and gyms ("Customers") use the Service to record the attendance of their students, employees or members ("People"). Contact: {{ s.legal_email }}.

1. Roles

For the data of People, the Customer is the controller and we are a processor acting only on the Customer's documented instructions (see the data processing agreement). For the account data of Customer administrators we are the controller.

2. What we process

  • Profile data entered by the Customer: ID, name, group, optional e-mail and phone.
  • Biometric templates: from 3-5 photos taken at registration, the Service computes a numerical face template (128 numbers). Photos are not stored; templates are stored encrypted and are only used to recognize the person for attendance at that Customer. Templates are never shared between Customers and never used to train models.
  • Camera frames during attendance are processed in memory and discarded immediately.
  • Attendance records: date, check-in / check-out time, status, recognition confidence.
  • Account and technical data: administrator names, e-mail addresses, login logs.

3. Legal basis and consent

Face templates are special-category (biometric) data. The Service only allows registering a face after the Customer confirms that the person has given explicit, informed, written consent (see the consent form), and records the date of that consent. People must always be offered an alternative way to record attendance (e.g. manual registration) and can withdraw consent at any time.

4. Retention

Customers configure retention. By default, face templates are deleted automatically when a person has not been recognized for 365 days, and immediately when the person is deactivated or deleted. Deleting a person deletes all their data.

5. Rights of People

People can ask the Customer for access, a copy (the Service exports all data of a person as a file), correction, deletion, restriction, or withdrawal of consent. We help Customers answer such requests. People can also complain to their data protection authority.

6. Security

Encryption of templates (AES with authentication), hashed passwords, per-Customer isolation, role-based access, HTTPS, audit log of automatic decisions, backups. Staff access to Customer data is restricted to support requests by the Customer.
