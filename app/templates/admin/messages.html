Send SMS from a school phone (free)
  1. Install the FaceAttend Android app on a phone with a SIM card and SMS credit.
  2. In the app, open ⋮ → SMS sender (parent messages) → Scan pairing code, scan this code, then tap Start sending messages.
  3. Keep the phone on and connected to the internet or the school Wi-Fi: it sends the messages as normal SMS.
